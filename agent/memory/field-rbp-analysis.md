BREAKTHROUGH DATA (2026-06-15): user pasted docs/history.md = the crowd "Field" probabilities + realized If-Yes/If-No points for 8 settled matches (QAT/SUI, BRA/MAR, HAI/SCO, AUS/TUR, GER/Cur, NED/JPN, CIV/ECU, SWE/TUN = 78 markets). This is the FIRST time we can compute real RBP (the API never exposes crowd brier — see [[probability-cup-overview]]). Analysis: scripts/analyze_rbp.py + scripts/analyze_matchday.py; output data/rbp_analysis.csv.

CONFIRMED SCORING: RBP_market = 100*(crowd_brier - user_brier), where crowd_brier = (field_mean - o)^2 + Var(crowd). The Var(crowd) term (~+3.1/market avg) is an outcome-independent "dispersion bonus" banked by ANY player near the field. So a pure field-copier scores RBP = sum of that bonus.

KEY RESULT over the 78 markets:
- Our REALIZED RBP = +178.0 (positive, ~+2.28/mkt). BUT decomposes into crowd-variance BONUS +241.7 and our own SKILL (vs the field MEAN) = **-63.7**. We beat the field on exactly 39/78 = 50% of markets.
- A FIELD-COPIER would have scored +241.7 — i.e. our model COST us ~64 pts vs just predicting the consensus on this sample.
- Overall skill -63.7 is only -0.8 sigma (95% CI on mean skill [-2.83,+1.07] includes 0) — NOT significant overall.
- BUT the deficit is concentrated: GOAL-DERIVED families (total_goals, btts_and_total, team_goals_ge, team_scores_in_half, h2_more_goals) = -102.2 over 16 mkts = **-3.90 sigma (REAL signal)**. Strip goals out and the other 62 markets net **+38.5** (we BEAT the field).
- Best family: team_count_ge/offsides +31.9 (n=7). Also positive: total_count_ge/cards +24.9, team_win +8.7, more_sot, corners. SOT props: skill ~-0.7/mkt (break-even vs field) but realized +27.7 (high-variance markets). RECONCILIATION (why goals lose despite a good model): the LARGE-SAMPLE StatsBomb/club backtest (data/backtest_results.parquet, ~41k matches/family) shows our goal models ARE calibrated and beat base rates (every family edge>=0, bias<3pt) — but goal families have our THINNEST edge-over-base (+0.0088 vs +0.0138 for others). RBP is vs the FIELD, and the field is SHARPEST on goals/winner (most-bet, most-modeled quantity) and SOFTER on niche counts (offsides/SOT/corners/fouls). So our small goal edge is swamped by a sharp crowd, while our niche-count edge shows through. Goals aren't broken — we just shouldn't DEVIATE from the sharp consensus there.

STRATEGY (high-conviction, not overfit — backed by live -3.9sigma + large-sample + clear mechanism): (1) Anchor goal-derived + match-winner markets tightly to the de-margined odds/consensus; minimize Dixon-Coles half-split/combo deviations there. (2) Spend the "deviation budget" on soft markets where we have validated edge: offsides (biggest), SOT differentials, corners, fouls, card totals (+referee research). (3) Do NOT soften the SOT intl discount — diagnostic flipped this: fading the field pays (+17.1, 4/5 right), the discount helps; the real (small, n=3) leak is club-form forward overrides running hot ABOVE the field. Keep the discount; watch the forward overrides. (4) Don't make >15pt contrarian bets without family-validated edge (big deviations net -43.7 live). (5) HIGHEST LEVERAGE: capture the field probabilities pre-lock if visible on the platform — would let us flag/avoid value-destroying deviations and anchor efficient-market families to consensus.
