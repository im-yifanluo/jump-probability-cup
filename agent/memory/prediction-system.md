Full system built 2026-06-12 (user-approved plan, ~04:00-05:00Z). Pipeline: `scripts/price_match.py "CAN vs BIH" --save` → ESPN public API odds (DraftKings, summary endpoint `pickcenter` has moneylines; scoreboard buckets by US local date) → `ScorelineModel.from_market` (Dixon-Coles fitted to de-margined 1X2 + totals) → `CountPricer` (NB regressions of corners/cards/SOT/fouls on odds-implied lambdas, fitted on 116k club matches; offsides/penalty/red + all half-shares from 314 StatsBomb tournament matches) → `PlayerModel` (StatsBomb history shrunk to priors + data/player_overrides.yaml) → `MatchPricer.price(spec)` with backtest-derived blend weights (BLEND_WEIGHTS in pricer.py; cards w=0.40 — card markets have no team-quality signal).

Backtest (41k matches/family, walk-forward 2017-2026, `notebooks/02_backtest.ipynb`): all 19 families beat the 50% baseline, 16/19 beat informed base rates; pooled calibration within ±1%. Biggest edges: more_sot (+0.036 Brier vs base rate), team_win, more_corners, team_goals_ge2. Tournament base rates (StatsBomb): penalty 0.293/match, red 0.096, pen-or-red 0.369, goals 2H share 0.599, cards 2H share 0.648, offsides 1.56/team (alpha 0.162).

First live scores (KOR vs CZE, 5 of 10 settled): mean Brier 0.2241 vs 0.25 baseline. Schick SOT (66%, scored 0.44 — he had no SOT) shows lineup research matters; players flagged `[prior]` in price_match output need verification before submitting.

Calibration fix #1 — player SOT props (2026-06-13, after 3 games / 30 settled at mean Brier 0.247): player_sot_ge was the worst family (live Brier 0.305, worse than coin-flip). Root cause diagnosed via StatsBomb: (a) I fed club-season SOT rates (Schick 1.21, Džeko 1.16) → P(>=1)~0.68, but tournament play suppresses output (team SOT 3.70 tournament vs 4.41 club = 0.839 discount); (b) Poisson is correct conditional on the right rate (within-player overdispersion ~0; the 1.29 population ratio is between-player). Rebuilt src/models/players.py: club rates ×0.839 discount, shrink toward 0.72 forward prior (k=1), minutes factor (expected_minutes override), Poisson kept. Validated leave-one-tournament-out (scripts/validate_player_sot.py, regression test): Brier 0.176 vs old 0.212 vs 0.25 coin, n=4982. Stale-history fix: StatsBomb rates can be stale when a player's role changed (McTominay was a deep mid 2018-22, now goal-scorer) — daily_run now flags sparse StatsBomb (<8 apps) for current-form research via update_player_rates (API-Football current club SOT). Tempo multiplier capped at 1.6. Scorer family (player_scores) was already good (Brier ~0.10) — untouched. NOTE the live board's Schick/Džeko 66-68% are the SETTLED KOR/CAN games (frozen); fix applies to future matches.

Calibration fix #2 — first_goal_and_other_scores_2h combo (2026-06-13): was our single worst live miss (0.593) and used an independence approximation P(A first)×P(B 2H) that overstated the joint by ~0.02-0.03. Replaced in goals.py with a half-structure closed form (condition on whether game's first goal is 1H or 2H; for 2H use P(first 2H goal is A AND b2>=1) = a2/(a2+b2)(1-e^-(a2+b2)) - (1-e^-a2)e^-b2). Verified against Monte Carlo (agreement <0.005). Live combos now ~0.16-0.24, correctly lower for favorite-scores-first-and-weak-team-scores cases.

Calibration fix #3 — count-model international discounts + offside scaling (2026-06-13, after QAT vs SUI settled at 0.275, worse than coin flip). Diagnosed by validating the not-previously-backtested per-team count families against StatsBomb truth: (a) OFFSIDES were scaled by attack^0.5 but the measured offside-vs-attack elasticity is only 0.18 (corr 0.08 — offsides are defensive-line driven, not attack driven); fixed exponent 0.5→0.18 (OFFSIDE_TEMPO_EXPONENT), validated 628 team-matches (Brier 0.2447 vs 0.2472). (b) Count regressions are club-fitted but applied to tournaments which have different rates; added COUNT_INTL_DISCOUNT = cards 0.849, corners 0.872, sot 0.839, fouls 1.104 (tournaments more physical), measured club-vs-StatsBomb. CountPricer gained tournament=True flag (applies discounts); backtest sets tournament=False so club backtest is byte-identical (verified). Card 1+ in 2H neutral dropped 0.742→0.684 (StatsBomb truth 0.653); SUI card 0.72→0.62, SUI offsides 0.59→0.49 (both were live misses). NOT overfit to 1 game — all validated on 314-628 StatsBomb samples. SOT family now well-calibrated live (n=11, pred 0.52 vs actual 0.55). Live overall still ~0.25 (market-anchored markets can't beat the crowd; backtest 41k is the accuracy authority).

Calibration fix #4 — referee-adjusted card model (2026-06-14, after BRA vs MAR settled at 0.206, model's best game; 50 settled at 0.244 overall, below coin flip; SOT/offsides/corners all well-calibrated now confirming fixes #1-3). Cards remained the worst family (0.376). Root cause: referee strictness is the dominant card driver — StatsBomb shows total cards/match mean 3.46, std 2.16, range 0-19; a strict vs lenient ref swings P(4+ cards) from 0.24 to 0.66. Added data/referee_overrides.yaml ({match: {referee, cards_per_game}}) + CountPricer.card_factor = cards_per_game/3.46 (capped [0.6,1.6], moderated for intl context since domestic ref rates run higher). /matchday step 2c researches the appointed ref + their cards/game per match. First use: AUS vs TUR (Valenzuela, card-heavy CONMEBOL ref, factor 1.33 → 4+ cards 0.49→0.71), Haiti vs SCO (Ghorbal, factor 1.16). Defaults to neutral (1.0) when ref unknown. This is the highest-leverage card input and a likely crowd-edge (casual predictors ignore referees).

CRITICAL strategic finding 2026-06-14 — user reported we were ~200th on the leaderboard. Diagnosis: our live Brier (0.264 over 69 settled) is WORSE than always-0.5 (0.250) and much worse than always-base-rate (0.241) — the sophisticated model was SUBTRACTING value vs trivial baselines. Cause: count/prop models are OVERCONFIDENT because international group-stage football is far more random than the club data they were fit on, and the crowd (hedging toward base rates) beats us. The earlier reliability curve + monotonic-shrinkage-helps + now the leaderboard all agree (I was too statistically cautious declining shrinkage at n=60). FIX: added TOURNAMENT_WEIGHT_CAP=0.55 on SHRINK_FAMILIES (total_count_ge, team_count_ge, both_teams_count_ge, team_more_count_than, player_sot_ge) in pricer.py — caps their model weight, pulling toward the structural baseline (~base rate). Goal/odds-anchored families NOT shrunk (odds genuinely informative; live favourite-underperformance is variance that reverts). Baseline retains referee card_factor so the referee EDGE survives the shrinkage. Validated on settled data: Brier 0.264->0.256, count/prop 0.267->0.252. HONEST caveat: this stops the bleeding (toward not-losing) but doesn't make us WIN — calibration ~= matching the crowd ~= mid-pack. Winning needs genuine edges where the crowd is systematically wrong (referee cards is the one real candidate). Lesson: optimize for BEATING THE CROWD (RBP), not calibration-vs-truth; and the live leaderboard overrides backtest optimism.
